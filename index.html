Learning center — foundation

These are the concepts the future AI explanation engine should reference.

Market structure

Identify higher highs/higher lows in bullish conditions and lower highs/lower lows in bearish conditions. Do not treat every small candle move as a structure change.

Liquidity sweep

Price trades beyond a visible prior high or low and then reacts. A sweep alone is not an entry; it needs context and confirmation.

BOS / CHOCH

A break of structure can support continuation; a change of character can suggest a possible shift. Higher-timeframe context matters.

Fair value gap

An imbalance created by strong displacement. The tool should treat an FVG as an area of interest, not an automatic buy or sell.

Risk management

Define risk before entry. The system should reject setups whose planned risk or stop placement does not meet your rules.
