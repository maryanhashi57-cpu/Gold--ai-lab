How this strategy works

  • 15-minute trend: directional context, not a prediction.
  • 5-minute trend: filters countertrend entries.
  • 1-minute setup: looks for liquidity rejection or a strong continuation candle after it closes.
  • Risk: stop location comes from recent price structure; targets are provisional. Do not force a trade count or a fixed close time.
  • Testing: track false signals, slippage, broker spreads, session times and actual outcomes. Historical paper testing alone does not prove future profitability.
